How much does a week in Japan cost? A real 2026 budget (and the hidden costs)

💴 Straight numbers by travel style — plus the quiet costs that blow the budget.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • Per day (2026, excl. flights): budget ¥8,000–12,000, mid-range ¥15,000–25,000, luxury ¥30,000–50,000+.
  • A week, then: roughly ¥56,000–84,000 (budget), ¥105,000–175,000 (mid), or ¥210,000+ (luxury) — ground costs only.
  • The gap between tiers is almost all sleep and dinner. The weak yen has handed most Western visitors a ~25–30% discount, so 2026 is one of the cheaper times to go. Watch the hidden costs below.

"How much do I need?" has one honest answer: it depends almost entirely on how you sleep and eat. Sightseeing, trains and konbini snacks cost roughly the same for everyone; the tiers diverge on hotels and dinners. Here are real 2026 numbers, then the costs people forget to budget.

Mio's verdict

Set your budget from your room rate, then defend it from the hidden costs. Pick a nightly rate you're happy with (that decides your tier), keep food flexible (konbini + teishoku by day, one nice dinner), tap an IC card instead of buying passes you won't fill, and pay in yen. Do that and a mid-range week lands around ¥120,000–150,000 of ground cost — comfortable, without leaking money to the traps.

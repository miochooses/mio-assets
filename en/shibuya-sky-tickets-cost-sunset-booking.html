Shibuya Sky tickets: what it really costs — and the sunset trap

🌇 For anyone planning that iconic open-air rooftop shot and about to find out the sunset slot is sold out.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • Two prices, not one. Online, a normal daytime slot is ~¥3,000; the popular sunset / after-3pm slot is ~¥3,700. Buying on the day at the counter costs more and may be sold out.
  • The trap: the whole point of Shibuya Sky is the open-air rooftop at sunset — so those exact time slots go first, often days ahead. Turning up at 5pm hoping to walk in is how people miss it.
  • Mio's rule: if you want the sunset rooftop, book the timed slot in advance. If you just want "Tokyo from high up," the free Tocho at 202m does that for ¥0. Compare all Tokyo decks →

Shibuya Sky is the rooftop with the see-through edge and the giant sky-facing deck you've seen all over Instagram — 230m above Shibuya Crossing. It's genuinely one of Tokyo's best paid views. But most tourists book it wrong: they don't realise there are two price tiers, that entry is by timed slot, and that the slot everyone actually wants sells out first. Here's the money read.

What Shibuya Sky actually costs

Prices depend on when you buy and which time slot you pick. As a 2026 reference: an adult online ticket for a standard daytime slot is around ¥3,000, while the sunset / after-3pm window is around ¥3,700. Buying at the counter on the day is priced higher again — and only if slots remain. So the cheapest way in is also the way that guarantees you a spot: book online, in advance.

Same rooftop, different slot: ~¥3,000 day vs ~¥3,700 sunsetThe ~¥700 premium buys the golden-hour light — which is exactly why it disappears first. Decide your slot before you decide your price.

When Shibuya Sky is worth it — and when it isn't

Worth it if the open-air rooftop at sunset is a specific thing you want — that experience is unique in Tokyo and hard to fake. Not worth it if you just want "a high view of the city": the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building (Tocho) gives you 202m for ¥0, and the Skytree sells raw height. Paying ¥3,700 for a view you can get free, because you didn't know the free one existed, is the actual mistake here.

How to book the slot you want

Pick your date and time slot first (sunset if that's the goal), then buy the cheapest ticket for that slot — which is almost always the advance online one. If the official site's sunset slots are gone, resellers and travel-activity platforms sometimes still hold timed inventory, so it's worth comparing before you give up on the sunset window or pay the higher counter price.

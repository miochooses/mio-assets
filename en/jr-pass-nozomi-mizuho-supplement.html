Your JR Pass does not cover the fastest bullet train — the ¥4,960 Nozomi trap

🚄 For JR Pass holders who assumed "unlimited Shinkansen" meant every Shinkansen.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • The pass excludes the two fastest trains. Nozomi (Tokaido/Sanyo) and Mizuho (Sanyo/Kyushu) are not included — board one without paying and you're travelling without a valid ticket.
  • The supplement is ~¥4,960 each way Tokyo–Shin-Osaka (varies by distance; same price for reserved, non-reserved and Green Car).
  • You almost never need it. The Hikari — fully covered by your pass — does Tokyo–Osaka in ~3h vs Nozomi's ~2h27m. You'd pay ~¥9,920 round trip to save ~30 minutes each way. Is the JR Pass even worth it? →

"Unlimited bullet trains" is the JR Pass pitch — but there's an asterisk most tourists miss. The Nozomi and Mizuho, the fastest services on the Tokyo–Osaka–Hakata corridor, are carved out of the pass. You can ride them, but only after buying a supplement. Here's the number, and why you should usually just take the free train instead.

Buy the "Nozomi/Mizuho Ticket" supplement at a JR ticket office; the price is the same whether you pick reserved, non-reserved or Green Car, and it scales with distance. Board a Nozomi on just your pass and you'll owe the fare plus the express charge on the spot.

The math: ~¥9,920 to save ~1 hour

On the classic Tokyo–Osaka run, Nozomi saves roughly 30 minutes over the free Hikari. Round trip, the supplement is about ¥4,960 × 2 = ¥9,920 — to save around an hour total. For most itineraries that's a poor trade: the Hikari runs frequently, has the same seats, and costs you nothing on top of the pass.

With a JR Pass: ride the free Hikari (~3h) not the ¥4,960 Nozomi (~2h27m)Pay the supplement only when a tight connection or a specific departure genuinely needs the fastest train. Otherwise the ~30 min isn't worth ¥4,960 each way.

Mio's verdict

Don't assume your JR Pass covers the fastest train — it doesn't. Ride the Hikari (or Sakura on the Sanyo line), which are fully included and only marginally slower. Reserve a seat for free through the pass and you get the same comfort. Pay the ~¥4,960 Nozomi/Mizuho supplement only when timing truly demands it — a tight same-day connection, or the single departure that fits your plan. For everyday city-to-city hops, keep the ¥9,920 in your pocket.

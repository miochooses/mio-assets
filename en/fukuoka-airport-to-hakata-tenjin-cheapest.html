Fukuoka Airport → the city for ¥260 in 5 minutes — Japan's closest airport, and the terminal catch

✈️ For anyone landing at Fukuoka (FUK) wondering how to reach Hakata or Tenjin without overpaying — spoiler: it's almost free and fast.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • Fukuoka Airport is the closest major airport to a city centre in Japan — about 3 km from Hakata Station. The city subway (Kūkō/Airport Line) runs right from the airport.
  • Subway to Hakata: ~5 minutes (2 stops), ~¥260. To Tenjin: ~11 minutes, ~¥260. Trains every few minutes. No taxi or express needed.
  • The catch: the subway station is under the domestic terminal. If you land international, take the free shuttle bus (~10 min) to the domestic terminal first — don't grab a taxi thinking there's no train.
  • Buses also run (some direct from the international terminal to Hakata/Tenjin for a similar fare); a taxi is quick but far dearer.

Most Japanese airports sit far from the city and cost a small fortune to leave. Fukuoka is the opposite: it's practically in the city — roughly 3 km from Hakata Station, the closest major airport to a downtown in Japan (arguably the world). Mio doesn't guess — here's how to leave it for pocket change.

Just take the subway

The Fukuoka City Subway Kūkō (Airport) Line starts right at the airport and runs straight into town:

  • To Hakata Station — about 5 minutes (just two stops), roughly ¥260. Hakata is the Shinkansen hub, so you can connect onward immediately.
  • To Tenjin (the main shopping/nightlife district) — about 11 minutes on the same line, roughly ¥260.
  • Trains run every few minutes, and you can tap an IC card (ICOCA/Suica etc.) or buy a ticket.
FUK → Hakata: ~¥260, ~5 minThere is essentially no cheaper or faster mainstream option — the subway wins outright.

The catch international arrivals must know

Here's the one thing that trips people up: the subway station is under the DOMESTIC terminal, and Fukuoka's international terminal is on the other side of the runways. If you arrive on an international flight:

  • Take the free shuttle bus from the international terminal to the domestic terminal (roughly 10 minutes), then go down to the subway.
  • Or catch a bus that runs directly from the international terminal to Hakata/Tenjin (similar low fare, a bit slower).

The mistake is landing international, seeing no train right there, and grabbing a ¥1,500+ taxi — when a free shuttle + a ¥260 subway was minutes away.

Mio's read

Fukuoka is the rare Japanese airport where the transfer is a non-event: the subway to Hakata is ~¥260 and ~5 minutes, to Tenjin ~11. Skip the taxi and the pricey extras. The only thing to remember is the international-terminal shuttle — take the free bus to the domestic terminal for the subway, or a direct city bus. Tap an IC card and you're downtown before you've finished checking your messages. Fares change — confirm on the day.

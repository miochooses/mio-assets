Mt Fuji day trip from Tokyo: ¥5,000 DIY or ¥12,000 tour?

🗻 For anyone who wants the Fuji photo and isn't sure whether to wing it by bus or book the tour.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • DIY is genuinely cheap: the express bus from Shinjuku (Busta) to Kawaguchiko is ~¥2,000–2,500 each way — call it ¥4,000–5,000 round trip, ~1.5–2.5h each way. Perfect if you just want the lake + one viewpoint.
  • A guided day tour is ~¥6,000–15,000 — more money, but it strings together 3–4 spots (5th Station, Oshino Hakkai, Lake Kawaguchi, sometimes Hakone) that are painful to chain by public transport in a single day.
  • Mio's rule: want one spot, your own pace, lowest cost → DIY bus. Want to see the most of Fuji in one day with zero logistics → the tour is the better value, not just the easier one. Cheapest way to Kawaguchiko →

"Mt Fuji day trip from Tokyo" hides a real fork: the raw transport is cheap, but Fuji's best spots are spread around the mountain and terribly connected to each other by bus and train. So the honest comparison isn't "bus vs tour price" — it's "one spot by bus" vs "several spots by tour." Here's the money read.

DIY: the cheap, single-spot option

The Fujikyu / highway express bus from Shinjuku's Busta terminal to Kawaguchiko is the budget backbone: roughly ¥2,000–2,500 one way, and it drops you right at the lake with the classic Fuji-over-water view. You can also go by JR + the Fujikyuko line, but that usually costs more and adds transfers. For a relaxed "see the lake, take the photo, eat lunch, come back," DIY is hard to beat on price — around ¥4,000–5,000 all-in on transport.

The tour: more money, but it buys reach

A guided day tour runs roughly ¥6,000–15,000 depending on what's included (lunch, the Subaru Line 5th Station, Oshino Hakkai springs, a Lake Kawaguchi cruise or ropeway, sometimes a Hakone leg on the way back). The thing you're actually buying isn't comfort — it's distance covered. Reaching 3–4 of those on your own in one day means tight bus timetables and a lot of waiting; the tour converts that into a fixed price.

One spot your way: ~¥4,000–5,000 · four spots, no logistics: ~¥6,000–15,000If you'd only ever reach one spot solo anyway, DIY wins. If you'd try to reach three and burn the day on buses, the tour is the cheaper use of both money and time.

Mio's verdict

Be honest about how many Fuji spots you'll really chase. If the answer is "one good lake view and a nice lunch," take the ¥4,000–5,000 bus and keep the rest. If it's "I want the 5th Station, the springs, the lake, maybe Hakone, and I don't want to decode bus timetables," the tour at ¥6,000–15,000 is the better value — you'd spend as much in time and missed connections doing it solo. The only real mistake is paying tour money and then doing one spot, or going DIY and wasting the day in transit.

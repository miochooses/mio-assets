New Chitose (CTS) to Sapporo: do you need the U-Seat upgrade?

✈️ For anyone flying into Hokkaido's New Chitose airport who wants the fastest-cheapest way into Sapporo.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • The JR Rapid Airport ("快速エアポート") is the move: ~33–37 min to Sapporo Station for ¥1,230 in an unreserved seat, roughly every 10–15 minutes.
  • You don't need the reserved "U-Seat" — it adds ¥800 (ticketless online) to ¥1,000 (paper) on top. Nice for a guaranteed seat with luggage at a busy hour, but the unreserved cars cost nothing extra.
  • Since the 2026 bus-fare revision, the train is also the cheapest option overall (buses take longer in traffic). Grab an unreserved car and keep the surcharge. Sort your yen first →

New Chitose (CTS) is Hokkaido's main gateway, and the money decision is refreshingly simple — as long as you don't over-buy the seat. The JR Rapid Airport runs straight into Sapporo, and the only real choice is unreserved (¥1,230) versus a reserved U-Seat that costs extra for the same ride.

Fares/times as published for 2026 (JR Hokkaido); confirm on the day. The Rapid Airport runs about six times an hour in the daytime. The U-Seat surcharge is ¥1,000 for a paper reserved-seat ticket, or ¥800 for JR Hokkaido's ticketless online reserved seat — both on top of the ¥1,230 base fare.

Unreserved ¥1,230 vs U-Seat ¥1,230 + ¥800 = ¥2,030Same train, same arrival time — the ¥800–1,000 only buys a guaranteed reserved seat.

When the U-Seat is worth it

The surcharge buys a reserved seat with a bit more space — genuinely nice if you land at a peak hour with big suitcases, or you're travelling as a group and want to sit together. Otherwise, the unreserved cars get you to Sapporo in the same 30-odd minutes for ¥1,230. Off-peak or travelling light? Skip it.

Mio's verdict

Take the JR Rapid Airport, ride an unreserved car for ¥1,230, and only add the U-Seat when a guaranteed seat is worth ¥800–1,000 to you. It's the fastest and — since the 2026 bus revision — the cheapest way into Sapporo, so there's rarely a reason to overthink it. Then spend the saved coins on a bowl of Sapporo miso ramen.

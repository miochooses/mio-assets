Arashiyama: the ¥0 bamboo trap vs the ¥880 train worth booking

🎋 For anyone who thinks Arashiyama is a "free day" and is about to skip the parts actually worth paying for.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • The bamboo grove is free — which is exactly why people treat all of Arashiyama as free and miss the two things worth money.
  • The Sagano Romantic Train (Torokko) is only ~¥880 one way for a 25-min scenic ride along the gorge — cheap, memorable, and it sells out (reserved seats; autumn especially). The Hozugawa river boat is ~¥6,000 for a ~2-hour float back.
  • Mio's rule: book the ¥880 Torokko in advance — best value in Arashiyama. Add the ¥6,000 boat only if you want the slow river experience, not just transport. Getting around Kyoto cheap →

Arashiyama gets sold as a free half-day: walk the bamboo grove, snap the photo, done. The grove is free — but the assumption that the whole area is free makes people skip the one paid thing that's genuinely worth it, and overthink the one that's optional. Here's the money read on what to actually book.

What's free (and enough for many people)

The bamboo grove, the riverside, the Togetsukyo bridge, and wandering the streets cost nothing. If your plan is "see the bamboo, take photos, eat lunch," Arashiyama is a ¥0 day plus food. Temples like Tenryu-ji add ~¥500–800 if you want the gardens. No need to pre-book any of that.

The ¥880 thing you should book: the Sagano Romantic Train

The Sagano Romantic Train (Torokko) is an open-window sightseeing train that runs ~25 minutes along the Hozugawa gorge — one of Arashiyama's best experiences, and at ~¥880 one way it's absurdly good value. The catch: seats are reserved and it sells out, especially in autumn foliage season. Turning up same-day and hoping for a seat is how people miss it. This is the one Arashiyama ticket worth locking in ahead.

Arashiyama can be ¥0 or ¥7,000+ — same placeFree = bamboo + bridge + streets. The ¥880 Torokko is the highest-value add-on; the ¥6,000 Hozugawa boat is worth it only if the slow 2-hour river ride is the point for you.

The ¥6,000 optional: the Hozugawa river boat

The Hozugawa River Boat Ride is a ~2-hour traditional boat float from Kameoka back down to Arashiyama, roughly ¥6,000 per adult. It's lovely but it's a slow experience, not efficient transport — pick it because you want the river, not to save time. Many people do the Torokko one way up and either the boat or a quick JR train back down.

Mio's verdict

Don't treat Arashiyama as a purely free day and wing it — the ¥880 Torokko is the highest-value thing in the whole area, and it's the one that sells out. Book that. Treat the ¥6,000 boat as an optional slow experience, not a must. Everything else (bamboo, bridge, streets) stays free. The real mistake is arriving with no Torokko seat and paying nothing memorable, or booking the ¥6,000 boat expecting it to be quick transport.

Miyajima's floating torii is nearly free — the ~¥21,000 is getting there from Kansai

⛩️ For anyone eyeing the floating torii as an easy day trip from Osaka or Kyoto.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • On the island it's cheap. Ferry ¥180 each way, the new ¥100 visitor tax, and Itsukushima Shrine ~¥300 — you see the famous floating torii for under ¥800.
  • Getting there from Kansai isn't. From Osaka/Kyoto it's a ~¥21,000, ~10-hour round trip: Shinkansen to Hiroshima (~¥10,000 each way), a local train, then the ferry.
  • The tax nobody mentions: since October 2023 there's a ¥100 per-visit visitor tax to enter Miyajima by boat (a ¥500 one-year pass exists). Small, but it's collected on top of the ferry. More day-trip math →

Miyajima's floating torii gate is one of Japan's iconic sights — and the sight itself is almost free. The expensive part, like the snow monkeys, is the distance. Here's what the island actually costs, the new tax most guides skip, and whether to day-trip it from Kansai or base in Hiroshima.

On the island: under ¥800 to see the torii

On MiyajimaCost

See the floating torii forunder ¥800

The ¥100 visitor tax is charged when you enter the island by boat; a ¥500 one-year pass is available if you're a repeat visitor. Walking around the island and viewing the torii from the shore is free — you only pay the shrine to go inside.

From Kansai: a ~¥21,000, ~10-hour day

The catch is the journey. From Shin-Osaka it's about ¥10,000 each way on the Shinkansen (~1.5 hours) to Hiroshima; from Kyoto a bit more. Then it's the JR Sanyo Line to Miyajimaguchi (~25 min, ¥420) and the ferry. Round-trip, that's a committed near-full-day out.

Same torii, very different day: on-island under ¥800 vs Kansai day trip ~¥21,600, ~10 hrsThe view is nearly free — you're paying for how far Miyajima is from Osaka. Base in Hiroshima (or route through it) and the day gets far cheaper and calmer.

Mio's verdict

Don't burn a whole day and ~¥21,000 to day-trip Miyajima from Osaka or Kyoto unless it's a real highlight for you. The smart play is to stay a night in Hiroshima (pair it with the Peace Memorial Park) or route through it on a wider western-Japan trip — then Miyajima is a cheap, relaxed half-day. Budget the small extras — the ¥100 visitor tax and ~¥300 shrine — and go early to beat the crowds and the tide for the classic "floating" shot.

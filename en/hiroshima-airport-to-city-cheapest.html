Hiroshima Airport to the city: the ¥1,500 bus (there's no train)

✈️ For anyone landing at Hiroshima Airport (HIJ) expecting a train into town — there isn't one, so here's the cheapest reliable way in.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • There is no railway to Hiroshima Airport. It sits ~50 km east of the city, so the way in is the airport limousine bus.
  • Bus to JR Hiroshima Station (Shinkansen north gate): about 50 minutes, ¥1,500 adult / ¥750 child, unreserved seating. Buses meet flights from stop no. 2.
  • Don't take a taxi — it's a ~50 km trip, so the fare runs many times the bus. Some buses also run to the Hiroshima Bus Center (near the Peace Park). IC cards / passes here →

Most big Japanese airports have a train or monorail into the city — so travellers land at Hiroshima and go looking for the station platform. It isn't there. Hiroshima Airport (HIJ) was built inland, about 50 km east of the city, and the only practical public route in is the airport limousine bus. The good news: it's cheap, direct, and timed to flights.

Which bus stop — station or bus center?

Two useful destinations: JR Hiroshima Station (best if you're catching a Shinkansen or staying near the station), and the Hiroshima Bus Center in the Kamiyacho/Hatchobori area (handy for the Peace Memorial Park, Miyajima ferries via tram, and many downtown hotels). Both are around the same ¥1,500 range; pick by where you're sleeping, not by a few yen.

Mio's verdict

Take the airport limousine bus for ¥1,500 — there's no train, and it's far cheaper than a ~50 km taxi. Choose the departure that matches your base (station vs bus center), keep a little time buffer because seating is unreserved, and put the taxi money toward okonomiyaki.

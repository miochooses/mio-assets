Kyoto to Nara: the ¥760 train — and why the ¥720 one can cost you

🦌 For anyone day-tripping from Kyoto to Nara (the deer, Todaiji) who wants the cheapest smart way there.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • Two lines run it. Kintetsu ordinary express → Kintetsu-Nara: about ¥760, ~35–50 min, and the station is only ~5 min from Nara Park.
  • JR Miyakoji Rapid → JR Nara: about ¥720, ~45 min — ¥40 cheaper, but JR Nara is a ~15-min walk to the park.
  • Don't auto-buy the Kintetsu Limited Express (~¥1,280 with the ¥520 surcharge). The free ordinary express is nearly as fast. Use JR only if you hold a JR Pass →.

The "cheapest" Kyoto→Nara ticket looks like the JR Rapid at ¥720. But the number people forget is the walk on the other end: JR Nara is ~15 minutes from the deer, while Kintetsu-Nara is ~5. For a day trip to Nara Park, the ¥40 you save on JR is quietly spent on a longer walk each way.

When each one wins

Kintetsu ordinary express is the default for most visitors — cheapest door-to-door to the deer, no surcharge. Take JR if you have a JR Pass (then it costs you nothing), or if your Kyoto hotel is right by Kyoto Station and you're comfortable with the longer Nara-side walk. Pay for the Kintetsu Limited Express only if you specifically want a guaranteed reserved seat on a busy day.

Mio's verdict

Take the Kintetsu ordinary express for ~¥760 — it lands you ~5 minutes from Nara Park and skips the surcharge. Don't chase the ¥40 JR saving into a longer walk, and don't pay ¥1,280 for the Limited Express unless you want the reserved seat. Save the difference for shika-senbei (deer crackers).

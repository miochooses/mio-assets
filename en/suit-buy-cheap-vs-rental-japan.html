Need a suit in Japan? Buy a cheap one vs rent — which is cheaper for you?

👔 For newcomers in Japan facing a dress code: job-hunting (recruit suit), a wedding or ceremony, or a sudden funeral needing black formal wear.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • It's a break-even question, not a taste question. A cheap chain suit ≈ ¥15,000–30,000 and you own it; renting ≈ ¥5,000–15,000 per occasion.
  • Rough rule: if you'll wear a suit 2+ times, buying usually wins. For a genuine one-off (a single funeral or one ceremony on a short stay), renting can win.
  • The case foreigners get caught by: Japan expects specific formal wear — a recruit suit for job-hunting and black mourning wear (mofuku) for funerals. A cheap owned dark suit covers most of it. See the full arrival checklist →

This is exactly the kind of "I'll just deal with it" decision that quietly costs you money — or leaves you underdressed at a moment when Japan really notices. Mio doesn't care about vibes; here's the math.

The two costs, side by side

A cheap suit from a chain (Aoki / Aoyama / Konaka style) is often ¥15,000–30,000 for an entry set, and it's yours forever. A rental runs roughly ¥5,000–15,000 per occasion (jacket, trousers, shirt, tie — often shoes too), illustrative and varying by grade and season. Renting looks cheap once; it stops being cheap the second time.

The trap most people miss: the funeral case (mofuku)

Funerals in Japan come with almost no notice and a strict expectation: plain black formal wear. Strictly, mofuku (deep-black mourning wear) is more formal than an everyday dark business suit, and it's something you'll rarely need. Buying a full formal set you might wear once in years is often a waste — this is the clearest case where rental wins, and many rental shops can deliver overnight for exactly this emergency.

Smart middle path: own one plain dark suit (covers job-hunting, ceremonies, and most everyday formal needs), and rent proper mofuku only if a funeral demands it. That collapses the "buy a rarely-used formal set" cost while keeping you covered day to day.

Mio's verdict

Wearing a suit 2+ times → buy a cheap chain suit. A true one-off, especially a funeral → rent. The "right" answer isn't about looking sharp — it's about how many times the meter runs.

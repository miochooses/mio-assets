Mio uses illustrative ordinary-car reserved fares and nationwide ordinary pass prices. Regional passes (e.g. JR East, JR West Kansai-area, JR Kyushu) are often much better value if you stay in one region — this tool only compares the nationwide pass. See the article for regional options.

What this means for you

Two things flip most travellers' decision. First, the Nozomi/Mizuho catch: the nationwide pass doesn't cover Japan's fastest Tokaido/Sanyo trains without an extra fee, so your real travel days can be slower — worth knowing before you optimise around it. Second, a single city base: if you fly into Tokyo and mostly day-trip, point-to-point almost always wins; the pass earns its keep on long, multi-city routes (Tokyo → Kyoto → Hiroshima → Fukuoka and back).

You can pay ¥3,000+ for a Tokyo skyline — or see it free from the Tocho

🌆 For anyone about to book a pricey Tokyo observation deck without knowing there's a free 202m one.
⏱ 30-second answer
  • The Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building (Tocho) observatory is FREE. Two towers, 202m up, no reservation — a genuine city panorama (Shinjuku) for ¥0.
  • Paid decks sell an experience, not just a view: Shibuya Sky ~¥3,000 (¥3,700 from 3pm) for an open-air rooftop; Tokyo Skytree from ~¥1,800 for the tallest vantage (450m Galleria costs more).
  • Mio's rule: if you just want "see Tokyo from high up," go to the Tocho for ¥0. Pay only for a specific thing — Shibuya Sky's edge-of-rooftop, or Skytree's raw height. Getting around cheap →

Every Tokyo itinerary lists Shibuya Sky and the Skytree as must-pay attractions. They're great — but the assumption that a skyline view costs ¥3,000 is wrong. Tokyo hands you one of its best panoramas for nothing. Here's the money read on all three.

Start with the free one: the Tocho

The Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building in Shinjuku has two observation decks at 202m, open to the public free of charge, usually with no reservation needed. On a clear day you can see across the city — and on a very clear day, Mt Fuji. For a first "wow, Tokyo is huge" moment, it does the job the ¥3,000 decks do, at ¥0. Go here first; decide later if a paid deck adds enough to be worth it.

What the paid decks actually sell

Shibuya Sky isn't really about being high — it's about the open-air rooftop: standing at the edge with the city 230m below, best at sunset (which is exactly why the after-3pm slot costs more). Tokyo Skytree sells height — 350m on the main deck, 450m on the Galleria — the tallest view in the city. Neither beats the Tocho on "just a view"; they win on the specific experience.

Mio's verdict

Do the Tocho for ¥0 first — for most people that's the skyline box ticked. Then pay for one paid deck only if you specifically want that experience: Shibuya Sky for the open-air rooftop at sunset, or the Skytree for sheer height. Don't buy all three; that's ¥6,000+ for three versions of the same city.
